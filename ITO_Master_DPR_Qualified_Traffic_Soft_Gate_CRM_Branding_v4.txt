PROJECT MANDATE
Executive Summary
India Trade Overseas requires a measurable B2B customer-acquisition system in which advertising does not end at a
website click. The target operating model is: qualified traffic → product-specific landing experience → requirement
capture → value-based soft gate → identified lead → CRM → qualification → quotation → order → revenue → repeat
business.
CRITICAL CLARIFICATION
No legitimate website or advertising system can guarantee that 100% of visitors will submit personal details
or buy. This DPR instead defines a 100%-testable technical standard: every successful lead submission must
be persisted, attributed, routed, auditable and recoverable; campaign scaling is prohibited until end-to-end
QA passes.
The current live website already has a strong B2B positioning, multiple product verticals, a commercial enquiry process
and visible trust markers. The key conversion weakness is that the Stone experience asks for Full Name, Email, Phone,
City and State before presenting substantive product value. The general contact and quote forms are also heavy for cold
paid traffic. The proposed architecture keeps the premium institutional brand while replacing early hard gating with
progressive qualification and a commercial-value soft gate.

1. Current Live Website Audit
The live homepage positions India Trade Overseas as a B2B sourcing, bulk supply and export company from India. It
lists Agriculture, Stone, Coal, Industrial Materials, Consumer Products and Logistics; its process asks buyers to share
product, quantity, destination and delivery requirement. The homepage also displays APEDA, DGFT, ISO, FSSAI, GST,
IEC and MSME/UDYAM trust markers.
 Homepage H1 is commercially clear: B2B sourcing, bulk supply and export from India.
 The crawlable homepage contains the B2B Sourcing / Bulk Supply / Documentation Support / Logistics Coordination
block twice; IT should remove duplicate rendering/content.
 The footer exposes Stone as a crawlable link while several other business verticals appear as button elements;
strategic product pages should use normal crawlable anchor links.
 The live Stone page opens with an identity gate asking Full Name, Email, Phone, City and State before useful product
detail.
 The current Contact page asks buyer type, contact person, company, email, mobile/WhatsApp, product/service,
quantity, destination, required-by date, specification, payment terms and commercial requirement—good for
serious corporate intake, too heavy as the first conversion step for cold paid traffic.
 The current Request a Quote page asks Full Name, Company, Phone/WhatsApp, Email, Product/Service,
Quantity/Volume, Destination and additional requirements—appropriate for high-intent quote requests, not the only
lead-capture mechanism.
 The /our-services route currently serves essentially the same content as the homepage and should be rebuilt as a
genuine services hub.
 Google Tag Manager is visibly embedded on audited pages; public crawl alone cannot verify whether GA4, Meta
Pixel, Conversions API, event mapping, deduplication and CRM attribution are correctly configured.
P0 DECISION
Do not scale another major Stone traffic campaign into the existing entry gate. Keep the brand. Rebuild the
paid-traffic conversion path.

100% workable does not mean every visitor converts. It means every defined system component is testable
and reliable: successful lead submissions are persisted, attributed, routed or safely queued; critical events are
verified; sales outcomes are recorded; and no major campaign is scaled while the funnel is technically blind.
 No silent lead loss.
 No serious paid campaign without tested attribution.
 No critical funnel step without an event definition.
 No lead without owner/status.
 No lost opportunity without reason.
 No scaling decision based solely on clicks or CTR.
 No paid product ad to a mismatched generic landing page.
 No mandatory identity gate before basic product value.
 No unverified claim of certification, price, stock or delivery.
 No release without end-to-end mobile QA.
