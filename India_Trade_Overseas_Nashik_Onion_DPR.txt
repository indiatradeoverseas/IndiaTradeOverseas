PART V - OPERATIONS
Quality Assurance, Storage and Logistics
The operating system must protect the buyer-approved specification at each handoff - lot selection, grading,
packing, loading, transport and documentation.
Five-stage quality protocol
Stage Checks
Supplier and lot Identity, origin, crop, availability, preliminary size, quality and transit suitability
Pre-packing Colour, firmness, skin, neck, size, sprouting, decay, cuts, doubles and foreign matter
Sorting and packing Removal of unsuitable bulbs, size segregation, weight, count, labels and packing
strength
Pre-dispatch Quantity, bag count, vehicle or container condition, ventilation, loading and seal
Independent inspection Buyer representative or approved third party when mutually agreed
Storage and handling
● Use dry, clean and ventilated storage protected from rain and standing water.
● Avoid excessive stacking pressure and unnecessary handling.
● Separate wet, damaged or decayed bulbs and monitor stock condition.
● Apply first-in, first-out movement and plan dispatch against crop condition.
● Assess storage suitability against curing, humidity, temperature and expected holding period.
Logistics controls
● Domestic: truck selection, freight, route, loading point, driver details, tracking and proof of delivery.
● Export: container availability and condition, vessel cutoff, customs, loading pattern, seal, Bill of Lading and
arrival updates.
● Do not guarantee a fixed payload before confirming bag size, product density, container type, legal payload
and carrier rules.

Buyer Questions and Objection Handling
Do you supply across India?
Yes. Supply may be coordinated according to quantity, destination, vehicle availability and agreed commercial
terms.
Do you export Nashik onions?
Yes, subject to Indian export policy, destination requirements, product availability, logistics and mutually
accepted payment terms.
What is the minimum order?
It depends on destination, packing and delivery structure. Domestic transactions are generally commercial
truckloads; export orders are generally container-based.
Can the buyer select size?
Yes. Requirements such as 25+, 35+, 40+, 45+, 50+ or 55+ mm may be considered according to lot
availability.
Can the buyer inspect before dispatch?
Yes. A buyer representative or approved third party may inspect when mutually agreed in advance.
Can loading evidence be shared?
Photographs, videos, bag counts, sample-weight checks and seal details may be shared according to the
confirmed process.
Are CIF quotations available?
EXW, FCA, FOB, CFR and CIF may be considered according to transaction and destination feasibility.
Can shelf life be guaranteed?
No universal shelf life should be guaranteed because performance depends on crop, curing, handling, storage,
humidity and transit.
Can an annual fixed price be offered?
Recurring supply can be discussed, but a market-linked agricultural product normally requires a defined
price-review mechanism.

Management principle

from campaign source through qualification, quotation, Purchase Order, delivery and repeat business.
