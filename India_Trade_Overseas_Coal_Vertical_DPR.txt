01 | Executive summary
India Trade Overseas will create a dedicated Coal & Industrial Materials vertical that combines
premium cinematic presentation with disciplined B2B lead capture. The page will explain the four
origin categories, help buyers state the correct calorific-value basis, collect the complete technical
requirement and convert qualified interest into an actionable commercial enquiry.
The recommended positioning is sourcing, bulk supply, trade and logistics coordination. The page
must not imply mine ownership, unrestricted allocation, guaranteed stock or fixed specifications
unless those claims are supported by current documents.
Business objectives
• Make the coal offering understandable within five seconds.
• Generate verified enquiries containing origin, GCV basis, quantity, application and destination.
• Present Assam, Jharkhand, Indonesian and U.S. coal without mixing incompatible testing bases.
• Build credibility through transparent specifications, process and documentation.
• Integrate the page with CRM, analytics, campaign attribution and disciplined follow-up.
• Create search visibility for domestic and imported coal requirements.

Four origin-led coal offerings

05 | Assam coal
Assam coal is positioned as a high-calorific domestic option with low-ash characteristics in official
mineral literature. Sulphur can be comparatively high, so suitability must be confirmed through a
current mine declaration, laboratory report or certificate of analysis before any application-specific
commitment.
Reference grade Official source-specific GCV band
G1 Above 7,500 and up to 7,600 kcal/kg
G2 Above 6,700 and up to 7,000 kcal/kg
G4 Above 6,100 and up to 6,400 kcal/kg
Reference: North Eastern Coalfields, Tikak Colliery. These are source-specific reference grades, not a standing ITO stock
guarantee.

Jharkhand coal
Jharkhand coal spans multiple coalfields, mines, seams and product categories. It must not be
marketed under one universal GCV. Non-coking coal should be identified by the applicable G1-G17
grade; coking coal must be evaluated primarily through ash and coke-making parameters.
Potential category Required commercial description
Non-coking thermal coal Mine/source, declared GCV grade, basis, size and COA
Steam/industrial coal Boiler application, GCV, ash, sulphur, moisture and size
ROM or sized coal Source permission, sizing, oversize/undersize tolerance and delivery
Washed coal Yield/source, ash guarantee, moisture and test method
Coking/metallurgical coal Ash plus full coking analysis; GCV alone is insufficient
Indonesian coal
Indonesian thermal coal should be presented using GAR, or Gross As Received, unless a different
basis is expressly agreed. The Indonesian government's HBA framework provides useful benchmark
reference specifications, but those values are not automatic specifications for every cargo.

HBA III 3,400 kcal/kg 44.30% 0.24% 3.88%
Reference: Indonesia Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources, Ministerial Decision 227.K/MB.01/MEM.B/2023. Benchmark
reference only.

SPECIFICATION GOVERNANCE
GCV basis, test data and offer control
08 | Calorific-value terminology
Term Meaning Commercial implication
GCV Gross Calorific Value / Higher Heating Value Must state the analytical basis
NCV Net Calorific Value / Lower Heating Value Lower than GCV because latent heat is excluded
GAR Gross calorific value on as-received basis Common in imported thermal-coal trade
NAR Net calorific value on as-received basis Common commercial comparison metric
ARB As-received basis Includes the condition and moisture at receipt
ADB Air-dried basis Cannot be compared directly with GAR without conversion data
DB Dry basis Moisture removed for analytical reporting
DAF Dry ash-free basis Excludes moisture and ash; not a delivered-energy figure
MANDATORY WEBSITE NOTE
A statement such as 5,500 kcal/kg is incomplete unless the page, enquiry or quotation also states
GAR, ARB, ADB, DB, NAR or another mutually accepted basis.

COMMERCIAL JOURNEY
Application, sourcing and delivery
09 | Industries and applications
Coal selection must reflect the buyer's plant design, fuel-handling system, environmental
permissions and process requirements. The website should invite enquiries from the following
approved commercial uses without representing universal suitability.
• Thermal and captive power generation
• Industrial boilers and process heating
• Cement, sponge iron, steel and rolling-mill requirements
• Brick, paper and tea-processing industries
• Industrial trading, distribution and stockyard supply
Six-stage sourcing-to-delivery process
Step Stage Action
01 Requirement Buyer submits origin, GCV basis, quantity, application and destination.
02 Review ITO checks supply-channel, quality, documentation and logistics feasibility.
03 Offer Available grade, price basis, taxes, freight, validity and terms are documented.
04 Verification Source documents, COA, inspection and sampling are agreed where applicable.
05 Dispatch Loading, weighment, transport and dispatch documentation are coordinated.
06 Closure Delivery records, documents and commercial closure are completed.
Quality and documentation
• Commercial quotation and proforma invoice
• Purchase order and supply/sale contract
• Source declaration and certificate of analysis
• Weighment slip, invoice, e-way bill and transport document
• Railway receipt, bill of lading, origin, inspection and insurance documents where applicable
Logistics capability language
India Trade Overseas coordinates coal movement according to source, shipment size, destination
and feasibility through road, rail-linked dispatch, stockyard movement, port handling, bulk vessel
shipment or multimodal arrangements. All logistics commitments remain subject to transporter,
railway or port acceptance, permits, route conditions, weather and final written confirmation.
